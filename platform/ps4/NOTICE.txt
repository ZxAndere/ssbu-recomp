SSBU Platform Diagnostic - experimental homebrew, no game content.

This project's sources are under the GNU GPL version 3 (see licenses/project.txt).
Source and build scripts: https://github.com/ZxAndere/ssbu-recomp
Upstream runtime: https://github.com/Denkishi/ssbu-recomp

Built with OpenOrbis PS4 Toolchain v0.5.4:
https://github.com/OpenOrbis/OpenOrbis-PS4-Toolchain/releases/tag/v0.5.4
The generic icon, right.sprx (credited to IDC by OpenOrbis), libc.prx and
libSceFios2.prx come from that release's hello_world sample. They are not from
the user's console or game backup. OpenOrbis license: licenses/openorbis.txt.
Static libc: OpenOrbis/musl, copyright Rich Felker and contributors;
license and source attribution: licenses/musl.txt and
https://github.com/OpenOrbis/musl

Toolchain/host utilities remain outside the package. No Nintendo assets,
generated game code, firmware files, DLC, encryption keys or save data are
included. This application does not launch or emulate Smash Ultimate.
